Our Story

One spit. One family. No shortcuts.

How a single stall turned into the order everyone in the neighbourhood keeps coming back for.

Dewaan means a gathering place — and that's exactly what our counter has become. We started with one skewer, one recipe passed down through the family, and a simple rule: nothing gets served until it's grilled to order. Years later, the rule hasn't changed, even as the line at the counter has grown.

Every morning starts the same way — vegetables chopped fresh, sauces made from scratch, and the marinade started a full day ahead so the meat has time to actually take on flavour. By the time the spit is turning at noon, everything on the menu is ready to be rolled the moment you order it.

How We Cook

Grilled to order, every single time.
