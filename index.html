Our Story

Built on one spit, one family, one standard.

Dewaan Shawarma started as a single stall with a single promise: everything gets grilled to order, never held under a lamp. That promise still runs the kitchen today — from the flatbread we toast per wrap to the pickles we cut fresh every morning.
